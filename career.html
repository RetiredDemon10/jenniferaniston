Career

Film & Television

From sitcom darling to dramatic powerhouse — every era of an extraordinary screen career.

The Small Screen

Television Landmarks

1990 — 1994 · The Grind

The Lean Years

Before fame: bit parts, failed pilots and four short-lived series (Molloy, Ferris Bueller, The Edge, Muddling Through). The resilience built here became her superpower.

1994 — 2004 · NBC

Friends

236 episodes as Rachel Green — runaway bride, Central Perk waitress, fashion executive, mother. Five Emmy nominations and one win; one of the greatest sitcom performances ever given.

2019 — Present · Apple TV+

The Morning Show

Alex Levy — veteran anchor fighting for her seat, her voice and her soul. The role earned Aniston a SAG Award and some of the finest reviews of her career, season after season.

The Big Screen

Selected Filmography

Filter by genre and explore the movies — from cult comedies to quietly devastating dramas.

1993

Leprechaun

Tory Reding

Her first film — a horror-comedy she later called a rite of passage. Every legend starts somewhere.

1997

Picture Perfect

Kate Mosley

An advertising executive invents a fiancé to get promoted — a sparkling early lead at the height of Friends mania.

1999

Office Space

Joanna

The cult classic about staplers and soul-crushing cubicles — and the server who just wants to express herself.

2002

The Good Girl

Justine Last

A quietly devastating turn as a small-town woman suffocating in her own life — proof, early, that the drama was always there.

2003

Bruce Almighty

Grace Connelly

Opposite Jim Carrey at his chaotic peak — a massive global hit and one of her biggest box-office smashes.

2004

Along Came Polly

Polly Prince

Free-spirited Polly upends a risk-averse man's world — cementing her reign as Hollywood's rom-com queen.

2006

The Break-Up

Brooke Meyers · Producer

A couple breaks up but neither will leave the condo. Aniston also produced — the start of her life behind the camera.

2008

Marley & Me

Jenny Grogan

A family, a dog, and fifteen years of life. One of her most beloved films — and her biggest box-office opening ever.

2009

He's Just Not That Into You

Beth Murphy

An ensemble dramedy about modern love in Baltimore — anchored by her quietly heartbreaking storyline.

2010

The Bounty Hunter

Nicole Hurley

A reporter is chased across state lines by her ex-husband — action, banter and chemistry in equal measure.

2011

Just Go with It

Katherine Murphy

Adam Sandler's co-conspirator in a web of little white lies — a $200M+ worldwide hit and pure crowd-pleasing fun.

2011

Horrible Bosses

Dr. Julia Harris

Fearless and hilarious as a dentist with no boundaries whatsoever — a against-type comic villain people still quote.

2013

We're the Millers

Rose O'Reilly

A stripper posing as a suburban mom in a fake family RV caper — her biggest live-action comedy hit.

2014

Cake

Claire Bennett

Bare-faced and broken as a woman living with chronic pain. A Golden Globe nomination and a career-defining performance.

2019

Murder Mystery

Audrey Spitz

A hairdresser and her husband stumble into a yacht-bound whodunit — one of Netflix's most-watched original films.

2023

Murder Mystery 2

Audrey Spitz

The Spitzes go full-time detective — and straight to the top of Netflix charts around the world once again.
