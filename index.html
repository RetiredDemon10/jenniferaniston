The Woman Behind the Icon

America's Golden Girl, Reinvented

Jennifer Joanna Aniston was born on February 11, 1969, in Sherman Oaks, Los Angeles, into a family of actors. What began with off-Broadway stages and short-lived sitcoms became, in 1994, the role of a generation: Rachel Green on Friends.

Ten seasons, an Emmy and a Golden Globe later, she refused to be typecast — building a formidable film career in comedy and drama, and then, in 2019, delivering the performance of her life as Alex Levy in The Morning Show, earning her first dramatic Screen Actors Guild Award.

“I’m a realistic optimist.”
