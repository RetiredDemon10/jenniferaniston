Philanthropy

The Heart Behind the Fame

A closer look at Jennifer Aniston’s long-standing support for children, health, equality, disaster relief and animal welfare.

Quiet Consistency

Giving Without the Spotlight

Jennifer Aniston’s charitable work has often unfolded without seeking attention. Across film sets, campaigns, telethons, auctions and private donations, she has supported causes involving seriously ill children, cancer research, hunger relief, disaster response and civil rights.

Her philanthropy is not defined by one signature campaign. It is a long pattern of lending her platform, time and resources to organizations working where help is needed most.

“There is nothing more rewarding than making someone else’s life better.”
