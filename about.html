Biography

The Life of Jennifer

Actress, producer, businesswoman — and one of the most recognizable faces on the planet.

Early Life

Born into the Spotlight

Jennifer Joanna Aniston was born on February 11, 1969, in Sherman Oaks, Los Angeles, the daughter of actors John Aniston and Nancy Dow. Her father, best known for his long-running role on Days of Our Lives, gave her a front-row seat to the business — and a healthy respect for how hard it truly was.

She moved to New York as a child, trained at the Fiorello H. LaGuardia High School of Music & Art and Performing Arts, and worked as a waitress and telemarketer while auditioning. Her early years were a masterclass in rejection: Molloy, Ferris Bueller, The Edge and Muddling Through all came and went. She has said she was nearly ready to quit acting when one final audition changed everything.

That audition was for a sitcom then called Friends Like Us.

Timeline

A Career in Moments

1969

Born in Los Angeles

Jennifer Joanna Aniston arrives on February 11 in Sherman Oaks, second daughter of actors John Aniston and Nancy Dow.

1989

The Move to Hollywood

After stage work in New York and off-Broadway productions, she relocates to Los Angeles to pursue television.

1994

Rachel Green Is Cast

Aniston joins six unknowns for a new NBC sitcom called Friends. The pilot airs September 22 and television is never the same.

1995

“The Rachel” Sweeps the World

Her shaggy, layered haircut becomes the most requested style of the decade — a cultural phenomenon with her name on it.

2002

The Emmy

After ten seasons of escalating brilliance, Aniston wins the Primetime Emmy for Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series.

2003

The Golden Globe

She adds a Golden Globe for Best Actress in a Television Series — Musical or Comedy to her collection, capping the Friends era.

2004

The Finale

Friends ends after 236 episodes, watched by more than 50 million viewers. Aniston turns fully to film — and to producing.

2012

A Star on Hollywood Boulevard

She receives the 2,462nd star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame — directly in front of the iconic Roosevelt Hotel.

2014

“Cake” & Critical Acclaim

Her unglamorous, gutting performance as Claire Bennett, a woman in chronic pain, earns a Golden Globe nomination and her best reviews in years.

2019

Alex Levy

Aniston returns to television as anchor Alex Levy in Apple TV+'s The Morning Show — and reminds the world what she can do.

2020

The SAG Award

She wins the Screen Actors Guild Award for Outstanding Female Actor in a Drama Series for The Morning Show.

2021

The Reunion & LolaVie

The Friends: The Reunion special streams worldwide, and she launches LolaVie, her hair and body care brand.

Today

Still Evolving

As The Morning Show continues into new seasons, Aniston remains a producer, entrepreneur — and endlessly watchable.

Beyond Acting

Producer, Entrepreneur, Philanthropist

Aniston co-founded Plan B Entertainment and later Echo Films, producing projects including The Switch, Call Me Crazy: A Five Film and Dumplin'. In 2021 she launched LolaVie, a haircare line reflecting her decades of working with the world's best stylists.

She is also a committed philanthropist — a long-time supporter of St. Jude Children's Research Hospital, Stand Up to Cancer, Feeding America and disaster relief efforts, using her platform quietly and consistently.

Named People magazine's "Most Beautiful" twice (2004 and 2016) and among its most intriguing people, she has spent three decades in the public eye without ever losing her warmth — or her wit.
