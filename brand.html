The Brand

Not Just a Name on a Bottle

LolaVie is Jennifer Aniston’s haircare brand, launched in 2021 after years of development. The name combines Lola, a nickname connected to her early California life, with Vie, meaning life.

Aniston is involved in product development, creative direction and marketing. The brand’s philosophy is intentionally focused: high-performance, multifunctional formulas made with clean, naturally derived ingredients.

Rather than launching a crowded product line all at once, LolaVie introduced formulas gradually, beginning with the now-iconic Glossing Detangler.
