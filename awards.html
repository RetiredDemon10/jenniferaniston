Recognition

Awards & Honors

Emmys, Golden Globes, SAG Awards — and a permanent place on Hollywood Boulevard.

The Wins

Milestone Victories

★
2002 · Primetime Emmy Awards

Outstanding Lead Actress in a Comedy Series

For the tenth and final season of Friends — the culmination of a decade as Rachel Green, after four previous nominations.

◆
2003 · Golden Globe Awards

Best Actress — Television Series, Musical or Comedy

The Hollywood Foreign Press honored her Rachel Green once more, in the year of the series' farewell.

✦
2020 · Screen Actors Guild Awards

Outstanding Female Actor in a Drama Series

For Alex Levy in The Morning Show — her first major dramatic acting prize, two decades after her comedy triumphs.

✺
1996 · Screen Actors Guild Awards

Outstanding Ensemble in a Comedy Series

The six Friends — Aniston, Cox, Kudrow, LeBlanc, Perry and Schwimmer — won together, as it should be.

Permanent Honors

A Place in History

✪
2012 · Hollywood Walk of Fame

Star No. 2,462

Unveiled on Hollywood Boulevard in front of the Roosevelt Hotel, honoring a film and television career spanning more than two decades at that point.

♥
2019 · People's Choice Awards

The People's Icon Award

A special honor celebrating a career that has charmed audiences across generations — from sitcom fans to a whole new era of streamers.

❦
2004 & 2016 · People Magazine

"Most Beautiful" — Twice, Twenty Years Apart

Named the world's most beautiful woman at 35 and again at 47 — a testament to grace that has only deepened with time.

✧
2021 · Emmy Nominations

Friends: The Reunion — Outstanding Variety Special

The reunion special she executive produced brought the six back together — and earned Emmy recognition as a variety event.

The Nominations

Also In Contention

◈
2015 · Golden Globe Awards

Best Actress in a Motion Picture — Drama

A searing nomination for Cake, honoring the raw, makeup-free performance that reintroduced her as a dramatic force.

◇
2020 · Primetime Emmy Awards

Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series

Her first-ever Emmy nomination in a dramatic category — for Alex Levy in the debut season of The Morning Show.

✩
2024 · Primetime Emmy Awards

Outstanding Lead Actress in a Drama Series

A second Emmy nomination for The Morning Show, arriving a remarkable 22 years after her win for Friends.

✦
2003 · Independent Spirit Awards

Best Female Lead — The Good Girl

Independent film's highest recognition for her haunting turn as Justine Last, a woman quietly coming apart in small-town Texas.
